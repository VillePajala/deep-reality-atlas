# Atlas Territories

A working map of conceptual territories the atlas claims and intends to develop.
This document exists so the scope is not lost to conversation decay. It is the
territory-of-the-territory — the atlas's own index of what it is mapping.

## Core (one sentence)

The atlas is an apophatic instrument — an attempt to document what consensus
reality omits, using drawing and text as joint evidence, with the cartographer
knowingly inside the territory.

Two things hold it together emotionally: the hauntedness (body, room, page,
apartment all registering more than they admit) and the 3am signal (the
frequency some people cannot stop receiving).

## The four clusters

### Cluster I — Underdeveloped / already-implied

Concepts present in the manifesto or existing journal but without their own
extended treatment.

1. **Death / bardo** — not death-as-metaphor but the cartographer's mortality
   as teacher. Tibetan bardo literature is phenomenologically specific:
   chikhai (the moment of dying), chonyid (the luminous visions), sidpa
   (the return to incarnation). Also: death as the edge that gives the atlas
   its urgency.
2. **Dreams as their own country** — the felt-texture of dreaming,
   recurring dreams, hypnagogic/hypnopompic threshold states (Mavromatis),
   lucid dreaming as apophatic discipline, the dream that will not leave.
3. **Madness as epistemology** — madness as a valid mode of knowing.
   Laing, Foucault's *Madness and Civilisation*, the shamanic initiatory
   crisis (the sickness required before healing others). The atlas's
   relationship to its own diagnosability.
4. **The wound (Chiron)** — the structural centre. The atlas-maker is an
   acupuncturist-artist making the atlas because something hurts. The
   wounded healer archetype. The wound that cannot be healed and therefore
   becomes the instrument.
5. **Time** — presentism, eternalism, Heidegger's Dasein-time, Proust's
   memory-time, the specious present, the gaps (see 1:00/1:15 entry).
   Non-linear time as the atlas's actual medium.

### Cluster II — Adjacent territory

Related concepts the atlas has not yet entered.

6. **Phantom limb** — Ramachandran. The body-map exceeds the body. The
   atlas as a phantom-limb of whatever is being drawn.
7. **Smell** — ghost sense, bypasses cortex, goes to limbic. Proust. Rooms
   that smell of what happened in them.
8. **Animals / other-than-human gaze** — Berger's *Why Look at Animals?*
   The moment non-human eyes return the gaze. Animals as teachers, not
   props.
9. **Synaesthesia** — cross-modal perception. Rimbaud's vowels, Kandinsky,
   Nabokov. A window onto normal perception's arbitrariness.
10. **The face** — Levinas's face-of-the-other, the uncanny valley,
    pareidolia. Faces in things. The ethical weight a face carries.
11. **Grief as a form of seeing** — Didion. Grief thins the world, and
    sometimes shows what ordinary perception occludes. Accidental mysticism.
12. **Place as a category** — Celtic thin places, genius loci, felt-place.
    Specific Helsinki corners with different density than others.
13. **The dead / hauntology** — Derrida. Presence-as-absence. Ancestry.
    The atlas works with ghost points without theorising ghosts.
14. **Prayer** — prayer as structural relation rather than request. People
    who pray without knowing to what.
15. **The idiot / holy fool** — yurodivy, Zen fool, Kierkegaard's knight
    of faith. The archetype the atlas-maker resembles.
16. **Ceremony and ritual** — why ritual works even when disbelieved in.

### Cluster III — Slightly extreme

Where the atlas goes out on limbs.

17. **Machines that dream (AI)** — the atlas-maker in 2026 cannot avoid
    this. Is the atlas threatened by, kin to, or orthogonal to computational
    minds? The Gnostic pneuma question under substrate-independence.
18. **Chronic illness as knowledge** — Sontag, Woolf's *On Being Ill*. The
    body that will not stop speaking.
19. **Children as temporary mystics** — what children see before the
    seven-year door closes. The atlas as an adult trying to remember.
20. **Desire / eros as cosmology** — Plato's *Symposium*, Lacan. The atlas
    is a love letter with footnotes. Naming this converts the project from
    inquiry to spiritual practice.
21. **Apophenia at the extreme** — pattern-matching as gift and pathology,
    the schizophrenic continuum. The ethics of being at the loud end.
22. **Mathematics as mysticism** — Pythagorean number-being, Cusanus's
    infinite, Grothendieck's final visions. The atlas's grids are already
    Pythagorean.
23. **Animism as rational position** — Abram, object-oriented ontology.
    Objects have interior. The desk is not inert.
24. **Mirrors** — Borges, Lacan's mirror stage. Why you cannot look in a
    mirror for long.
25. **Underworld / katabasis** — Persephone, Dante, Jung's *Red Book*.
    Helsinki apartment as descent space. Formal pattern of descent.
26. **Diseases of the soul without medical names** — acedia, weltschmerz,
    taedium vitae, saudade, toska, mono no aware. Vocabulary we have lost.

### Cluster V — Extreme territory

Where the atlas takes its largest risks. These are phenomena the dominant
discourse either medicalises, ridicules, or disappears. The atlas's
apophatic method makes it the right instrument for them: it can document
without endorsing, and refuse to explain without dismissing.

30. **Psychedelic phenomenology** — DMT's consistent cross-report entities
    (McKenna's machine elves), ayahuasca's perceived teachers, the
    question of whether consistent phenomenology across strangers is
    evidence of anything. Not endorsing use; treating reports as data.
31. **The UFO / contact phenomenon** — Vallée's control system hypothesis.
    Not literal extraterrestrials but as the modern Western category for
    numinous encounter that has bled into physical radar evidence.
32. **Out-of-body and near-death experience** — Pam Reynolds, the veridical
    perception cases, the consistent tunnel/light phenomenology. What the
    data is and what it is not.
33. **Past-life memory** — Ian Stevenson's forty years at UVA. Children
    who remember. Whether or not one believes, the data is unsettling.
34. **Telepathy and the shared dream** — Dunne's *Experiment with Time*,
    the precognitive dream logs, the Rhine work. The residue that refuses
    to resolve.
35. **The persistent cryptids of sleep paralysis** — the hat-man, the
    crone, the tall thin one, the visitor at the bedside — appearing
    independently across cultures. Why the same beings.
36. **Possession phenomenology** — anthropological possession across
    cultures. Being-ridden vs having-a-presence. What the ethnographic
    record actually says.
37. **Stigmata and bodily impossibilia** — Padre Pio. The body as stage
    for convictions it may not have authored.
38. **Doppelgängers and bilocation** — cases of witnessed duplicates.
    Goethe, Maupassant. The own-double sighting.
39. **The Tulpa tradition** — Tibetan thoughtform creation. Internet
    tulpas. The claim that intention can make a being.
40. **Voodoo death and curse mortality** — anthropologically documented
    death-by-belief. Lester's cases. The power of social conviction to
    kill.
41. **Mass psychogenic events** — the Strasbourg dancing plague, the
    Tanganyika laughter epidemic, Salem. What we share is stronger than
    we think.
42. **Time slips** — Moberly and Jourdain at Versailles, the Bold Street
    cases. Anachronistic encounters as a persistent class.
43. **Kundalini as rupture** — not the sanitised version. Gopi Krishna.
    The specific cases where an energetic awakening breaks the person.
44. **Non-human intelligences (not the UFO kind)** — octopus cognition,
    slime mold problem-solving, plant signalling, deep-sea alien senses.
    The biological evidence that intelligence is plural and substrate-
    indifferent.
45. **Neurotheology / the God-helmet** — Persinger. The specific
    temporal-lobe states correlated with felt-presence and religious
    experience. What this correlation means and does not mean.
46. **The bicameral mind** — Julian Jaynes. The claim that pre-Homeric
    humans experienced their thoughts as external voices. Whether true or
    not, it names a real structure of consciousness.
47. **Operative magic** — Dee, Agrippa, the Golden Dawn. The Western
    esoteric tradition as a set of practices that apparently produced
    effects, independent of whether their metaphysics was correct.
48. **The simulation / prison-world** — the Gnostic version, not the
    Silicon Valley version. The persistent intuition across traditions
    (māyā, saṃsāra, the demiurge's fabric) that the world is a made
    thing.
49. **Glossolalia and the death of meaning** — the moment at the limit
    where meaning collapses into sound. Pentecostal speaking-in-tongues,
    Zen koan shattering, the baby's pre-linguistic articulation.
50. **Ghost roads and the persistent places** — specific highways,
    houses, hospitals where the same thing has been reported by strangers
    for decades. What the convergence is evidence of.

### Cluster VI — Further extreme territory

Topics that push the atlas further into disputed, disreputable, or
unresolved terrain. Many of these have rigorous partisans and are
dismissed in polite discourse. The atlas's policy remains: document
without endorsing, refuse to explain without dismissing.

**Subtopic expansions within Cluster V:**

- **DMT — ego death, mantis beings, the integration problem** —
  McKenna's heroic dose, Strassman, the specific recurring personae,
  the phenomenology of the five-minute disintegration, the unanswerable
  question of what to do afterward.
- **Alien abduction** — Budd Hopkins, John Mack (Harvard psychiatrist
  who risked his career on the phenomenon), hypnotic recall and its
  reliability problems, the abductee community's self-understanding.
- **Life review in NDEs** — the sequential, ethical, empathic
  structure; the "feel what others felt" phenomenology; its resemblance
  to bardo literature.
- **Terminal lucidity** — dementia patients returning to full
  articulate clarity in the hours before death. A well-documented
  clinical anomaly with no accepted mechanism.
- **Birth marks matching past-life wounds** — Stevenson's strongest
  data class.
- **Remote viewing and Ganzfeld** — Ingo Swann, the Stargate program,
  Bem's published meta-analyses, the replication-reputation split.
- **Twin telepathy and crisis telepathy** — pain-at-a-distance, the
  known-at-the-moment-of-death phenomenon.
- **Multiple personality (DID) as possession's medical cousin** —
  same phenomenology, different framing, different outcomes.
- **Bilocation proper** — Padre Pio, Maria of Agreda, María de Jesús
  de Ágreda's alleged visits to seventeenth-century Texas.
- **Vardøger** — the Norwegian double that arrives before you.
- **The Philip Experiment** — the Toronto group that constructed a
  fictional ghost and was reportedly answered.
- **Broken heart syndrome** — Takotsubo cardiomyopathy, voodoo death's
  cardiological echo.
- **Anniversary deaths and the hidden schedule of the body.**
- **Havana syndrome** — the still-unresolved case class.
- **Koro, windigo, amok** — culture-bound syndromes and what they are
  evidence of.
- **Déjà vu, jamais vu, presque vu** — the micro-glitches of the
  temporal stream.
- **Meditation-induced distress** — Willoughby Britton's research,
  Cheetah House, the dark side of the contemplative turn.
- **The imaginal realm** — Henry Corbin's *mundus imaginalis*, neither
  material nor purely intelligible but its own domain.
- **Temporal lobe epilepsy and religious experience** — Dostoevsky,
  Joan of Arc, the specific overlap of neurology and sanctity.
- **Hearing Voices Movement** — the movement to reclaim auditory
  hallucination as experience rather than symptom.
- **Sigil magic and chaos magic** — Austin Osman Spare, Peter Carroll,
  magic as deliberately unbelieved technology.
- **The Mandela Effect** — mass mis-remembering and what the fervour
  around it is evidence of.
- **Reality glitches** — internet reports of moments when the world
  briefly misbehaved.
- **Xenoglossy proper** — cases where a person reportedly speaks a
  language they never learned, documented on audio.
- **Mediumship and the Ouija phenomenology** — trance mediumship,
  automatic writing, what makes the practice work even when the
  metaphysics is unbelieved.

### Cluster IV — Meta-territory

About the atlas itself.

27. **Why drawings and not only text** — Wittgenstein's "what cannot be
    said must be shown." The atlas's self-awareness about its hybrid form.
28. **Map as mnemonic device** — Borges's map-that-covers-the-territory.
    The map as love object.
29. **Photograph as ghost** — Barthes's *Camera Lucida*, the punctum. The
    atlas reproduced as digital images — what happens to a handmade mark
    when it is pixelated.

## Voice registers (for content generation)

- **Sane/academic** — clean prose, correct terminology, essay-fragment.
- **Clinical/calm** — case-note style, impossible observations reported flat.
- **Lucid-poetic** — prose-poem clarity.
- **Conversational** — short encounter, quoted dialogue.
- **Lucid-mad** — 3am voice, long unbroken sentences, insights breaking through exhaustion.
- **Deranged-poetic** — mangled terminology, invented words, internally consistent madness.
- **Scream / ALL CAPS** — short, intense.
- **Ecstatic** — hymn-form, rising, invocational.
- **Fragment / breath** — one or two lines.

## Policy

- A "thorough investigation" of each topic = 3–5 entries across voice registers.
- Core topics (wound, death, desire, dreams, time) get 4–5. Peripheral topics
  get 2–3.
- The collection is called The Atlas of Insanity. It is meant to be enormous.
  Add, do not prune. Let contradictions coexist.
