## FUTURE WORKFLOW (TODO — design later)

The production workflow should be: **journal entry + image = one unit of atlas content.**

Each new piece of the atlas is:
1. Write a journal entry (or batch of entries) in the madman's voice
2. Generate/render an accompanying image (Midjourney hero OR code-generated manuscript/infection/transmission)
3. Publish as a pair — the entry and its visual counterpart
4. The website grows one page at a time, like the atlas being drawn

This needs workflow design — tooling, templates, publication pipeline. But the principle is clear: text and image are inseparable. Each page of the atlas is both.
